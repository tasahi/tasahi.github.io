<section id="summary">
<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>PhD-level engineer (Electrical Engineering, MSc Physics) with over 20 years delivering software, modelling and machine-learning systems for industry, healthcare and research. I take technical problems from concept to deployed systems: Python backends and APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure on AWS, and the interfaces people use. My work spans Computer Vision integrated into physical and industrial systems, numerical simulation and optimisation, real-time sensor and telemetry analytics, and, more recently, production LLM agents and retrieval systems. I have led teams of up to 6 engineers and worked closely with clinicians, mining and industrial operators, and academic R&amp;D partners.</p>
</section>
